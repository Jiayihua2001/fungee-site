Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · Fungee is made by Jiayi Huang ("we", "us") · Questions: hjy3289616864@gmail.com

The short version

  • Your game lives on your phone. No ads, no tracking, and no account unless you join Friends.
  • Scans. When you scan a mushroom or a plant, and only after you allow it, the photo (or up to three photos of the same find) and a rough area (about 10 km) pass through our server to Anthropic's Claude for identification. We don't keep the photos.
  • What our server keeps. A random install ID and how many scans it made each day, so free identification can't be abused.
  • Steps are counted on your phone. They leave it only if you turn on the Friends steps board, which shares this week's total with friends who share theirs.
  • Friends (optional). If you join, our server keeps the name you choose, a friend code, who your friends are, the messages you send (phrases, stickers, mush cards, gifts, and typed words if you turn typing on), and the mushes you show in the gallery. Typing is for players 13 and over, between friends who both turn it on, and is filtered. There is no photo sharing. Deleting your data deletes all of it.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your payment details.

2. What stays on your phone

Your mushes, jars, items, songs, journal, the photos you keep in it, your settings and your step counts (from Motion & Fitness) are stored only on your device. So are the postcards your mushes send from their journeys, the place names on them, the photos of those places they're painted from, and the drawings, notes and tunes you make for sparks. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple includes them in your device backup. Deleting the app deletes them. If you share a postcard or a mush card, it goes wherever you choose to send it.

3. Photos and third-party AI (Anthropic)

Before your first scan, Fungee asks whether it may send scan photos to Anthropic's Claude. If you say no, you can still play, but scans won't be identified. You can change your answer at any time in Fungee: 📓 Journal, then ⚙️ Settings & privacy, then 📷 Photos to Claude.

Anthropic, PBC processes photos only to return an identification to us, as our service provider under its Commercial Terms of Service. Those terms bar it from training its models on this content. We don't send Anthropic your install ID or anything else that identifies you.

Please don't photograph people. If a person is in a photo, it is still sent and processed the same way.

4. Location

Location is optional. Fungee asks for it in the welcome screens, or the first time you scan or open the map. iOS lets you choose precise or approximate location. A precise position is used only on your phone, to show where you are on the hunt map: it is never stored or sent. Everything Fungee sends or saves uses your area rounded to about 10 km. If you decline, identification uses the photo and the month only, and nature facts use the Pittsburgh area.

The name you choose in the welcome screens stays on your phone, where Fungee uses it to greet you, unless you join Friends.

5. Friends (optional)

Friends is off until you join it from the 👥 Friends tab. Joining makes an account on our server without an email or password: it lives on your phone. Others can't search for you. A friend adds you by typing your friend code, so share it only with people you know.

Friends can send each other ready-made phrases, stickers, cards of their mushes and finds, and gifts from their bag. Friends can also type short messages to each other, but only if both have turned typing on. Before typing turns on, Fungee asks when you were born, and it stays off for anyone under 13 (the answer stays on your phone). Our server checks every typed message: at most 120 characters, no links, emails, @names, phone numbers or other long numbers, no invitations to other apps, and no rude words. Nobody can send a photo or a place. Names are checked against the same list of rude words and can't contain links, emails or long numbers.

The gallery shows mushes (their 3D look and name, never a photo) with their owner's name. Anyone can report a mush, a player or a typed message (🚩), and you can block anyone you've added. A report of a typed message keeps a copy of its words so we can read it. We read reports every day. A mush reported by three players is hidden until we review it.

6. Who else processes data for us

  • Anthropic, PBC: identification (Claude).
  • Supabase, Inc.: hosting our server and its database, in the United States (Amazon Web Services, us-east-1).
  • Apple: the App Store, payments and iCloud Backup, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Fungee also reads public nature facts, maps and photos from four open services. Like any website, they see your IP address, plus only what they need to answer. GBIF (the Global Biodiversity Information Facility) gets a rough area (about 10 km) and the month, to show what fruits near you. Wikipedia (the Wikimedia Foundation) gets the name of the species you're reading about. OpenFreeMap draws the map: like any online map, it sees which area you're looking at. When a mush goes on a journey, Fungee reads the names of nearby places for its postcard from the same map tiles, around your area rounded to about 10 km (place names © OpenStreetMap contributors). Wikimedia Commons (the Wikimedia Foundation) gets the name of a real place a mush visits and its map position, rounded to about 100 m, and nothing about you, to find a freely licensed photo of that place; Fungee keeps the photo on your phone to paint the postcard, credits its photographer, and Delete my data removes it. None of them gets your photos or your install ID.

We don't use advertising networks, analytics SDKs or data brokers, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

7. Children

Fungee is made for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Typed messages in Friends stay off for players who tell Fungee they are under 13; they can still use phrases, stickers and cards, which carry no personal information. Friends has no photos. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Your choices, and deleting your data

  • Stop sending photos to Claude: 📓 Journal, then ⚙️ Settings & privacy, then 📷 Photos to Claude.
  • Location, camera, Motion & Fitness: change them any time in iOS Settings → Fungee.
  • Delete data on your phone: delete the app, and remove its iCloud backup if you have one.
  • Leave Friends: Delete my data in Settings deletes your Friends account at once, with your friendships, messages, gallery mushes and likes. Reports other players made about you go too.
  • Delete data on our server: Delete my data in Settings removes your install ID from your phone, so our server's scan counts can no longer be tied to you. The server deletes them by itself 30 days after your last scan. It keeps them only under a scrambled form of the ID, so we can't find them from your name or email.
  • Anthropic's copy of past scans is deleted on Anthropic's own schedule (within 30 days, except as described in section 1).

9. Your rights

United States. Depending on your state (for example California, Colorado, Virginia or Connecticut), you may have the right to know what personal information we hold, and to correct or delete it. We don't sell or "share" personal information for targeted advertising, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights. To make a request, email us.

EEA, UK and Switzerland. We rely on:

  • Your consent for sending scan photos to Anthropic and for location. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Performance of our contract with you for identifying the scans you ask for and for Fungee+.
  • Our legitimate interests for security and abuse prevention.

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and you can object to processing. You can also complain to your data protection authority. Your data is processed in the United States.

10. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). We keep as little as we can, and only the people who run Fungee can access our server.
