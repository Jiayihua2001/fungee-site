Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Fungee is made by Jiayi Huang ("we", "us") · Questions: hjy3289616864@gmail.com

The short version

  • Your game lives on your phone. No ads, no tracking, and no account unless you join Friends.
  • Scans. When you scan a mushroom or a plant, and only after you allow it, the photo (or up to three photos of the same find) and a rough area (about 10 km) pass through our server to Anthropic's Claude for identification. We don't keep the photos.
  • What our server keeps. A random install ID and how many scans it made each day, so free identification can't be abused.
  • Steps are counted on your phone and never leave it.
  • Age. Fungee asks once when you were born and keeps only "under 13" or "13 and over". Under 13, Friends stays off until a grown-up turns it on, and postcards never carry a photo.
  • Friends (optional). If you join, our server keeps the name you choose, a friend code, who your friends are, the messages you send (phrases, stickers, mush cards, gifts, and typed words if you turn typing on), the postcards you send friends, and your tree house, which your friends can see and visit. Typing is for players 13 and over, between friends who both turn it on, and is filtered. Players 13 and over can add the original photo to a postcard for a friend who is 13 or over too. The phone crops it to the plant, removes its location, and sends it only if it finds no face, person or writing in it. Deleting your data deletes all of it.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your payment details.

2. What stays on your phone

Your mushes, tree house, furniture, items, songs, journal, the photos you keep in it, your settings, your age answer ("under 13" or "13 and over") and your step counts (from Motion & Fitness) are stored only on your device. So are the postcards your mushes bring home from journeys and visits (journeys go to made-up storybook places and use no location; cards from before 12 November 2026 may also hold the name and a photo of a real place nearby), the friends your mushes met on their journeys, your mushes' visits to each other's rooms, the copies of friends' tree houses your mushes visited, and the drawings, notes and tunes you make for sparks. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple includes them in your device backup. Deleting the app deletes them. If you share a postcard or a mush card, it goes wherever you choose to send it.

3. Photos and third-party AI (Anthropic)

Before your first scan, Fungee asks whether it may send scan photos to Anthropic's Claude. If you say no, you can still play, but scans won't be identified. You can change your answer at any time in Fungee: 📓 Journal, then ⚙️ Settings & privacy, then 📷 Photos to Claude.

Anthropic, PBC processes photos only to return an identification to us, as our service provider under its Commercial Terms of Service. Those terms bar it from training its models on this content. We don't send Anthropic your install ID or anything else that identifies you.

Please don't photograph people. If a person is in a photo, it is still sent and processed the same way.

4. Location

Location is optional. Fungee asks for it in the welcome screens, or the first time you scan or open the map. iOS lets you choose precise or approximate location. A precise position is used only on your phone, to show where you are on the hunt map: it is never stored or sent. Everything Fungee sends or saves uses your area rounded to about 10 km. If you decline, identification uses the photo and the month only, and nature facts use the Pittsburgh area.

The name you choose in the welcome screens stays on your phone, where Fungee uses it to greet you, unless you join Friends.

5. Friends (optional)

Friends is off until you join it from the 👥 Friends tab. Joining makes an account on our server without an email or password: it lives on your phone. Others can't search for you. A friend adds you by typing your friend code, so share it only with people you know.

Friends can send each other ready-made phrases, stickers, cards of their mushes and finds, and gifts from their bag. Friends can also type short messages to each other, but only if both have turned typing on. Typing stays off for anyone who told Fungee they're under 13. Our server checks every typed message: at most 120 characters, no links, emails, @names, phone numbers or other long numbers, no invitations to other apps, and no rude words. Nobody can send a place, and photos go only on postcards between players who are both 13 or over (below). Names are checked against the same list of rude words and can't contain links, emails or long numbers.

Friends can see each other's tree houses, and their mushes can visit: three visits a day, and one a day to each friend. A mush comes home from a visit with a postcard of the friend's tree house and the blueprint of one piece of furniture there. The friend sees who visited, and their first three visits each day bring them a few spores. A shared house carries which jar each room is in, each jar's playground and scenery, the furniture's names in the game, where each piece stands in its room, and the mushes' looks and names: no photos, places or your own name.

Friends can send each other postcards. A postcard has one of your nature stamps on the front, carried by one of your mushes, and on the back up to three ready-made phrases, or typed words if you and your friend both turned typing on (checked like typed messages). Your phone sends a small picture of the stamp's painting (about 440 pixels wide, with no photo data in it), the plant's name on the stamp, the carrying mush's look and name, and the note; our server adds both your names. A postcard arrives 20 to 40 minutes later. Only friends can send them, five a day and two a day to the same friend. Blocking someone stops their postcards and hides the ones they sent. You can give a card a ❤️ (its sender sees it), reply, take it out of your album, or report it.

Players 13 and over can add the photo a stamp was painted from, if the friend is 13 or over too. Before it can go, your phone crops it to the plant, makes it at most 1024 pixels wide, re-saves it so it carries no location or other photo data, and checks it with Apple's Vision for faces, people and writing. This check happens on your phone, and nothing is sent for it. If it finds any, or can't check, only the stamp's painting goes. A friend can see the photo only once the card has arrived, and no longer after taking it out of the album, reporting it or blocking you.

You can report a player, a typed message or a postcard (🚩), and you can block anyone you've added. A report of a typed message keeps a copy of its words so we can read it; a report of a postcard keeps its words and pictures. We read reports every day.

Fungee 0.8 and earlier also had a public gallery, a weekly steps board and friend battles. They're gone from the app. What you shared through them (gallery mushes and likes, step counts, battle teams and results) stays on our server until you delete your account, and we'll delete it when the last older version stops working.

6. Who else processes data for us

  • Anthropic, PBC: identification (Claude).
  • Supabase, Inc.: hosting our server and its database, in the United States (Amazon Web Services, us-east-1).
  • Apple: the App Store, payments and iCloud Backup, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Fungee also reads public nature facts and maps from three open services. Like any website, they see your IP address, plus only what they need to answer. GBIF (the Global Biodiversity Information Facility) gets a rough area (about 10 km) and the month, to show what fruits near you. Wikipedia (the Wikimedia Foundation) gets the name of the species you're reading about. OpenFreeMap draws the map: like any online map, it sees which area you're looking at. Journeys use none of them: since 12 November 2026 a mush travels to made-up storybook places. A photo of a real place kept for an older postcard stays on your phone, and Delete my data removes it. None of them gets your photos or your install ID.

We don't use advertising networks, analytics SDKs or data brokers, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

7. Children

Fungee is made for a general audience, including families who play together. When it first opens, it asks when you were born, without suggesting an answer, and keeps only "under 13" or "13 and over" on the phone. For a player under 13:

  • Friends stays off until a grown-up turns it on in 👪 For grown-ups, behind a question a young child can't answer.
  • There is no typing: only ready-made phrases, stickers, cards and postcards.
  • Postcards carry only the stamp's painting, never a photo.
  • The answer can't be changed to "13 and over" later.

With Friends on, our server keeps only what section 1 lists: a name the child chooses (Fungee suggests a nickname), a friend code, and what they share with friends added by code. We don't knowingly collect other personal information from children under 13. A grown-up can delete everything at any time from 👪 For grown-ups, or contact us to review or delete a child's information.

8. Your choices, and deleting your data

  • Stop sending photos to Claude: 📓 Journal, then ⚙️ Settings & privacy, then 📷 Photos to Claude.
  • Location, camera, Motion & Fitness: change them any time in iOS Settings → Fungee.
  • Delete data on your phone: delete the app, and remove its iCloud backup if you have one.
  • Leave Friends: Delete my data in Settings (for players under 13, in 👪 For grown-ups) deletes your Friends account at once, with your friendships, messages, postcards (the ones you sent, with their pictures and photos, and the ones sent to you), your tree house, every visit you made or received, your age answer, and anything left from the gallery, steps board and battles of older versions. Reports other players made about you go too.
  • Delete data on our server: Delete my data in Settings removes your install ID from your phone, so our server's scan counts can no longer be tied to you. The server deletes them by itself 30 days after your last scan. It keeps them only under a scrambled form of the ID, so we can't find them from your name or email.
  • Anthropic's copy of past scans is deleted on Anthropic's own schedule (within 30 days, except as described in section 1).

We don't sell or "share" personal information for targeted advertising, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights. To make a request, email us.

EEA, UK and Switzerland. We rely on:

  • Your consent for sending scan photos to Anthropic and for location. You can withdraw it at any time.

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and you can object to processing. You can also complain to your data protection authority. Your data is processed in the United States.
